# LANDING PAGE HERO SLIDER REQUIREMENT

Design a stunning premium landing page hero section with a crystal-clear bright image slider inspired by luxury fitness brands and modern SaaS websites.

The hero slider should feel:

* Premium
* Cinematic
* Energetic
* Bright
* Crystal-clear
* High-quality
* Luxury fitness inspired

Use:

* Ultra HD gym photography
* Bright crystal-clear visuals
* High contrast premium lighting
* Smooth animated transitions
* Modern overlays
* Elegant typography
* Orange glowing accent highlights
* Glassmorphism effects where suitable

---

# HERO SLIDER FEATURES

The landing page should include:

* Full-width fullscreen image slider
* Auto sliding transitions
* Manual next/previous controls
* Touch/swipe support for mobile
* Pagination indicators/dots
* Smooth fade or cinematic transitions
* Fast optimized image loading
* Lazy loading for performance

---

# SLIDER CONTENT

Each slide should include:

* Professional gym background image/video
* Motivational headline
* Short premium tagline
* CTA buttons

Example:
“Ignite Your Strength”
“Train Like an Athlete”
“Transform Your Body”

Buttons:

* Join Now
* Start Free Trial
* Explore Memberships

The slider must feel:

The final landing page should feel like a world-class luxury fitness website with crystal-bright visuals and premium cinematic experience.
